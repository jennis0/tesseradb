UNI B/e = unigram hybrid postings + front-coded dictionary (the shipped-design index).
BI B/e  = the same pair for token bigrams — option 2's whole additional cost.
TF B/e  = one u8 term frequency per (term, doc) entry — what a scored match adds.
POS B/e = exact delta-varint bytes of every token position — option 3's payload, excluding
          the posting-format change that is its real price.
Tokeniser is the probe convention (lowercase, [a-z0-9]+): adequate for cost shape on this
English corpus; vocabulary shape under a UAX#29 segmenter or on a CJK corpus is NOT measured.
