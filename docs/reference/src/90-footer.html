Every structure and constant on this page traces to the Tessera design corpus: the architecture design (the specification), the concurrency and lifecycle document (generations, pins, the three retirement rules, the write-ahead log), the contracts specification (bundle format and the plugin boundary), and the Phase 0 measurement record. Section references are given inline. Where the two disagree, the contracts specification's recorded deviations govern.

Measured figures were obtained on WSL2, 12 cores, 39 GB, over a real 2.42-million-paper arXiv corpus with synthetic access-control policies, scaled to 109. The measurement record describes its own timings as indicative rather than certified, and that caveat carries here.
