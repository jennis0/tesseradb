Tessera · seventeen plates and a figure board

Reading a masked corpus as a map

Tessera is an index for browsing a large document corpus as a pannable, zoomable map, under the requirement that a viewer’s permissions determine every count, density, cluster and label they are shown — not merely which items they can retrieve. Each plate below is self-contained and drawn to be lifted into a slide; measured figures are embedded where they belong, and machinery that is designed but not yet built is marked on the plate itself.
